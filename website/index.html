Portfolio analytics where the AI shows its work.

Faro computes risk metrics — Sharpe, Value-at-Risk, beta, drawdown — from their published formulas, then an AI copilot explains them in plain English or Spanish. A checker compares the numbers in each answer with the engine's results and flags the ones it can't match. It skips everyday values like 1 or 100, and a wrong number very close to a real one can pass. The methodology page lists its limits.

Why Faro

Serious analytics. Honest AI. Your machine.

Most AI finance tools ask you to trust a chatbot. Faro is built the other way around: deterministic math first, language model second, verification always.

🧮

First-principles quant engine

Every metric implemented from the documented formula and unit-tested against independently computed references — Sharpe, Sortino, alpha/beta, historical & parametric VaR, CVaR, max drawdown, correlations, concentration, risk contributions.

🔍

A copilot that shows its work

Ask "how risky am I?" or "what if NVDA drops 20%?" The agent is instructed to answer by calling the quant engine's tools. Every figure in its reply is checked against those computations, and numbers it can't trace are flagged.

🌎

Truly bilingual

Dashboard, copilot, scenarios, digests — everything works in English and Spanish with one tap. Ask in either language; get answers, number formatting and legal docs in yours.

⚡

Scenario lab & daily digest

Stress-test instant price shocks with per-position impact, and generate a one-click daily brief: movers, risk contributors, upcoming earnings — narrated from computed facts only.

🔒

Private & replicable

Your data lives in a local database on your machine. Clone → docker compose up → running in 5 minutes. Claude-powered when you add a key; free local model otherwise.

⚖️

Compliance-aware by design

Educational analytics, not advice. Faro never executes trades, never touches your brokerage, and is instructed to refuse "should I buy?" (a behavior exercised in the public eval) — showing you how to evaluate it yourself instead.

The core idea

Invented numbers aren't prompted away. They're caught and shown.

The #1 failure mode of LLM finance apps is invented numbers. Faro's answer is architectural — and it's measured, not assumed.

1

Deterministic engine underneath

Pure numpy/pandas implementations of every formula, documented and cross-checked against independent libraries in 97 tests.

quant/risk.py historical_var(returns, 0.95) → 0.0162 # unit-tested
2

The engine is the only approved source

The copilot is instructed to take every number from five typed tools that call the same service layer the dashboard uses: one engine, two consumers. Nothing forces it to, which is why the next step exists.

tools: get_metric get_position_detail run_price_shock_scenario compare_to_benchmark
3

Every reply is checked

A grounding checker extracts each number from the answer and matches it to this turn's tool results. Anything it can't trace is flagged in the app. It has blind spots, listed on the methodology page, and it's measured by a public two-mode bilingual eval with raw logs in the repo.

grounding_check: every number in the reply must trace to a tool result → flagged if not

Honest by two measures, not one flattering number

The eval runs two ways, and the repo publishes both with raw logs. Asked fresh, each answer's numbers trace to that turn's tools. In free-flowing multi-turn chat a small local model will recite earlier figures from memory — and every one is flagged in-app rather than hidden. Whether Claude does better is untested.
